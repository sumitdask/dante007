Research and Strategy

01

Direct Call Primacy

People searching for a flat tyre or a vehicle breakdown want an instant phone call, not a web form. Direct phone calls became the primary conversion action, with shop visits and lead forms tracked alongside.

02

3-Stream Intent Segmentation

Segmented user intent into three distinct funnels: emergency 24/7 mobile fitting, local tyre shop searches, and vehicle recovery. Each stream was allocated tailored ad messaging and dedicated landing pages.

03

100-Mile Regional Reach

Engineered regional targeting around Greater Manchester extending outward up to 100 miles, calibrated to align with client call-out pricing tiers based on distance from the dispatch base.

04

Channel Complementarity

Google Search Leads campaigns captured direct intent (12.44% conversion rate at £8.45 CPA), while Performance Max delivered massive local visibility and lowest unit conversion cost (£5.40).

Key Learnings

What worked: Three of the four active campaigns displayed "Limited by budget" status in Google Ads, demonstrating that significant uncaptured local demand exists across Greater Manchester at the £150/day level. Nearly all conversions were direct phone calls (the web lead form recorded just 1 conversion in the month), validating the phone-first strategy.

What did not: The 24hr mobile fitting campaign underperformed substantially, producing only 15 calls at an unacceptable £96.58 cost per conversion. The Tyre Shop campaign ran at £17.23 against a £15 target CPA with the highest CPC (£1.65). Performance Max delivered the lowest CPA (£5.40) but had a low 0.05% click-to-lead conversion rate, deriving substantial value from map impression volume and shop visits.

What I would do next: Restructure or pause the underperforming 24hr mobile fitting campaign and reallocate its daily budget into the high-converting Search Leads campaign (£8.45 CPA, 12.44% CVR). Furthermore, increasing overall daily budget from £150 to £200+ will capture high-intent local demand currently lost to budget constraints.
