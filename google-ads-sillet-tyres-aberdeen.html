Starting Point (Day 0)

  • No Ad Account: Zero prior Google Ads account, no historical search term data, and no call tracking infrastructure at launch on 1 April 2025.
  • Client's Core Goal: Generate steady, qualified phone enquiries for mobile tyre fitting, roadside assistance, and vehicle recovery rather than passive website clicks.
  • Constraints & Service Radius: Modest £40/day starting budget covering Aberdeen with plans to widen coverage in stages up to 50 miles.

Research and Strategy

01

Emergency Services Intent

Stranded motorists experiencing a puncture or breakdown require instant assistance. Phone calls convert orders immediately, making click-to-call the primary conversion vehicle over contact forms.

02

Intent-First Keywords & Negatives

Seeded campaigns around urgent "near me" and mobile-fitting phrases. Engineered a rigorous negative keyword list from search query data to block competitor brands, scrap yards, marketplaces, and irrelevant queries.

03

Phased Geographic Radius

Targeted drivers directly in and around Aberdeen city initially, then systematically expanded the radius outwards toward 50 miles in controlled stages as conversion density and dispatch times aligned.

04

Multi-Channel Channel Synergy

Allocated core capital to high-intent Google Search for premium call quality. Layered Performance Max later to capture supplementary local map and discovery impressions once Search conversion rates stabilised.

Key Learnings

What worked: Intent-first keywords, dedicated click-to-call mobile landers, and scaling in steps. Core Search campaigns carry the highest conversion quality, converting at 20–27%, while Performance Max adds volume at the lowest average CPC (£0.96).

What did not: Performance Max converts at roughly 1% per click, and much of its measured value represents estimated shop visits—requiring strict evaluation against verified phone calls. Breakdown service was the most expensive stream at £10.09 per conversion.

What I would do next: Shift budget from vehicle breakdown toward mobile fitting (which is currently limited by budget) and establish direct integration between Google Ads call logs and the garage's dispatch software to track booked jobs against calls.
