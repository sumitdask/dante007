Starting Point (Day 0)

  • No Account History: Zero prior Google Ads account, no historical search term data, and no digital lead capture pipeline.
  • Client's Goal: Secure urgent MOT bookings primarily through direct phone calls from local drivers searching on Google.
  • Constraints & Economics: Fixed monthly budget of around £1,200 (£40/day), single garage workshop location, and an MOT price of £34.99 requiring strict CPA efficiency.

Research and Strategy

01

Market & Urgent Local Intent

MOT testing is a legally mandated, high-urgency local service. Drivers usually search when their certificate is expiring within days, making immediate phone calls the fastest and highest-converting action.

02

Keywords & Booking Intent

Seeded campaigns around "MOT test" and tightly paired booking phrases ("book MOT Stoke", "MOT test centre"). Eliminated generic queries like "car repair" or "brake replacement" to protect budget.

03

Geofenced Local Targeting

Constrained delivery strictly to Stoke-on-Trent and surrounding ST postal sectors. Drivers will not travel unreasonable distances for an MOT, eliminating click waste from adjacent cities.

04

Channel Choice & Offer Hook

Google Search was chosen to capture active local queries. Performance Max (local) was introduced later to test incremental reach. The £34.99 MOT test price acted as a transparent, high-converting offer hook.

Launch and Learning Phase

Observation

Month 1 Learning Reality

In the first month, cost per call was around £7.00. This was the expected learning phase as the brand new account collected initial baseline data and auction insights.

Pivot on Day 2

Campaign Restructuring

Restructured the primary campaign on day 2 (5–6 March) to tighten keyword separation, isolate top-performing search terms, and establish a cleaner bid management structure.

Stabilization

Continuous Optimisation

Through regular negative keyword pruning, match type adjustments, and ad copy iterations, cost per call dropped into the £3.50–£3.95 range from May onwards and remained stable.

Key Learnings

What worked: Patience during the initial learning phase paid off immensely. Cost per call dropped by 46% once smart bidding gathered conversion volume, stabilising at £3.50–£3.95 from May through September.

What did not: While Performance Max delivered 50 additional calls in September, its conversion rate (9.41%) was under half that of Search (20.54%), and its cost per call was significantly higher (£6.08 vs £3.73). Dedicated Search remains the primary ROI engine for emergency local services.

What I would do next: The campaign frequently displayed "Limited by budget" status while maintaining £3.73 cost per call. Increasing daily spend from £40 to £60 would immediately capture untapped booking volume at sustainable unit economics.
